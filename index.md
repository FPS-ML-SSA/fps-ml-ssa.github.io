The **CORDEX Flagship Pilot Study on Machine Learning for Subtropical South America (FPS-ML-SSA)** aims to enhance climate downscaling at kilometre scale using machine-learning emulators of convection-permitting regional climate models (CPRCMs) driven by CMIP6.

Project information, methods, simulations, meetings, and publications will be added to this site as they become available.

## Project overview

- **Region:** Subtropical South America
- **Focus:** Kilometre-scale climate downscaling
- **Approach:** Machine-learning emulators of CPRCM-CMIP6 simulations
